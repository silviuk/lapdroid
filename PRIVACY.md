# Lapdroid privacy policy

Publisher: Silviu Vlasceanu. Last updated: 25 October 2026.

Lapdroid connects a Windows computer and an Android device over Bluetooth. No account is required. Lapdroid does not operate a cloud service or include advertising or analytics SDKs.

While you enable input capture, keyboard text, key events, mouse movements and actions are sent to the connected Android companion. The Android Accessibility service applies those actions to other apps. The optional Android input method forwards input to the focused app. Disable capture with F12 or the capture button when you want to return to Windows. Content entered into another app is subject to that app's privacy policy.

Bluetooth device names, addresses and connection information are processed to discover paired devices and establish the connection. Diagnostic logs can contain device identifiers and connection errors. Windows diagnostics are held in the app's memory; the Copy Logs control copies them to your clipboard only when you request it. Review logs before sharing them in a public issue.

Android Bluetooth permissions support connections. Overlay permission displays the pointer; Accessibility permission enables taps, gestures, navigation and text editing. The Android app does not request Internet permission. Device or operating-system backups and clipboard features are governed by your device settings.

Lapdroid does not send your input or device identifiers to the publisher. It does not sell personal information. External download, support and website links are handled by their respective providers.

To stop processing, disconnect the devices, stop input capture, disable the Android permissions, or uninstall the apps. For questions, contact the publisher through https://github.com/silviuk/lapdroid/issues. Do not include sensitive input or device identifiers in a public report.
